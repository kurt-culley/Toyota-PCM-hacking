# MR2 89661-17140: ignition maps in degrees

Generated by `python -m pcmre.readable17140 --write`. Do not edit.

Warm engine, no trims. **Rows: manifold pressure in kPa (absolute). Columns: rpm.**
Heavy load is at the top, like a tuning screen; 100 kPa is about wide-open throttle at sea level.

**How sure the numbers are:**
- **Degree scale:** 15/64° per map count, measured in the simulator at four speeds [EMU:tests/test_sim17140.py].
- **Absolute degrees:** taken from the ROM's fixed-timing value, assumed to be the factory 10° BTDC (GUESS). One timing-light reading in the car settles this.
- **kPa per row:** from the factory MAP-sensor curve (LIKELY) and the assumption that ADC channel 7 is the 5 V supply (GUESS).
