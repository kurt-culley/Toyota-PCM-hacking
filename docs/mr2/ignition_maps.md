# MR2 89661-17140: ignition maps in degrees

Generated by `python -m pcmre.readable17140 --write`. Do not edit.

Warm engine, no trims. **Rows: manifold pressure in kPa (absolute), on two scales. Columns: rpm.**
Heavy load is at the top, like a tuning screen; 100 kPa is about wide-open throttle at sea level.

**How sure the numbers are:**
- **Degree scale:** 15/64° per map count, measured in the simulator at four speeds [EMU:tests/test_sim17140.py].
- **Absolute degrees (LIKELY):** each cell is value × 15/64° BTDC. The anchor is the T-terminal check: with the throttle closed and T shorted the ROM uses a fixed sum, which the factory sets to 10° BTDC; off idle, warm and untrimmed, a map cell then lands within 0.04° of its own advance, and a warm idle with T open (idle table, not this map) sits at 16.0° BTDC [EMU:tests/test_ross17140.py::test_t_terminal_fixed_sum_only_with_the_throttle_closed]. Coolant and trim terms add to it. A timing light (about 16° at a warm idle with T open) confirms it ([`maps.md`](maps.md), STATUS conflicts log).
- **kPa per row, two scales** ([`maps.md`](maps.md), STATUS kPa-scale conflict, open). **LIKELY:** Load ≈ 6.14 × kPa + 123 (256 counts per 5 V), on which the top row is one atmosphere. **Simulator default** (first number): Load ≈ 4.98 × kPa + 98.6, which rests on the factory MAP-sensor curve and the simulator's GUESS converter constant `adc_k`. Load at key-on over the serial link settles it.

Between sites the ECU interpolates in both rpm and pressure. Outside the table it holds the edge values.
On a naturally aspirated engine the manifold cannot exceed about 100 kPa. On the LIKELY scale the top row of both maps is one atmosphere (101 kPa), so every row is used. On the simulator default the 117 and 130 kPa rows would never be reached, which is one reason that scale is doubted (maps.md).
